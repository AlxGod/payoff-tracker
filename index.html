Your numbers

A new goal applies to this month and future months. Past months keep the goal they had. The IRS sets a new mileage rate every January.

Backup

A backup is one small file with all your months, bills and trips. Save it to Files or iCloud Drive. If you lose your data or get a new phone, restore it here.

Bring in data from the old app

In the old Route to Zero app, open Settings and tap "Export this month as CSV", then pick that file here. Entries land in the right months, and anything already here is skipped.

Export a spreadsheet

Saves a spreadsheet file (CSV) of your earnings, spending and miles. Handy for taxes.

How your data is saved

Everything stays on this phone, inside this app. Nothing is sent anywhere and there's no account. Each entry is filed under the month it happened, so months start fresh on their own.

Always open it from your home-screen icon. On iPhone, the home-screen app and a regular Safari tab keep separate data.
